Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Who we are

BELIEVE Pharmacy [legal company name and registration details] runs this website and two pharmacy branches in Gulu, Uganda (Olya Road and Acholi Road). We are responsible for the personal information described here.

What we collect

  • Your account: name, phone number, email address and password. Your password is stored in scrambled (hashed) form by our sign-in provider, so we cannot read it.
  • Orders: what you order, delivery or collection choice, delivery address and the payment method you choose.
  • Prescriptions: the photo or file you upload, your name, phone number and notes. This is health information.
  • Pharmacist questions: the symptoms and details you send. They are sent to us through WhatsApp. This is health information.
  • Job applications: your CV, contact details and message.
  • On your device: your basket, saved products, light or dark mode setting and a saved copy of our product list, so the site loads faster. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking tools.

Why we use it

  • To process your orders and prescriptions and contact you about them.
  • To give you pharmacist advice you ask for.
  • To consider job applications.
  • To keep records and meet legal and professional duties for pharmacies.

For health information we rely on your consent, which you give when you send it to us. You can withdraw it at any time, but we may not then be able to help with that request.

Who can see it

Only our authorised pharmacy staff can open prescriptions, orders, pharmacist questions and CVs. Delivery staff see only what they need to deliver (name, phone and address). We never sell your information.

We use these service providers to run the site: Supabase (database, sign-in and private file storage), Vercel (website hosting), Google (Google Maps opens when you tap Get directions), WhatsApp (messages you choose to send us), Resend (emails that tell our staff a new order or prescription has arrived) and jsDelivr (delivery of site code). They process information for us or, in the case of WhatsApp and Google, under their own terms. We may also share information when the law requires it.

Where it is stored
